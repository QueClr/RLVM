# rlvm-machine/h10/force-inline.txt - methods that the native image inlines at every call site.
#
# native-image-args.sh adds them to -H:PriorityForceInline, after the continuation steps k_* that it finds
# itself. One "class.method" per line (every overload of the name); '#' starts a comment.
#
# Why a list: GraalVM Community Edition compiles ahead of time without a profile, so its inliner cannot
# tell a hot call from a cold one; on the JVM C2 inlines all of these by call count. Each entry was a
# call that showed in a profile of the executable (the cost of being called: frame, stack check,
# safepoint poll, spilled registers around the call). What is listed and what it is worth:
# *agentic-note*: rep-OPT-A.
#
# Rules for an entry:
#   - a leaf reader or a cache probe of a few lines whose rare part is a ..Slow method of its own, or
#   - a step that has one to three call sites in the hot path (inlining it adds no code), and
#   - never a method that is recursive through other entries (the inliner unrolls the cycle five times:
#     Infer.tmNode / Infer.tmApp grew from 1 kB to 41 kB each and bought nothing).
# Prefer an entry to a split of the method: an entry costs the JVM launcher nothing (the jars do not
# change), a part moved out of line is a call there, which C2 does not inline back.
# An entry of rlvm.machine that names no method of the machine classes is reported by the script and
# ignored; entries of other modules (rlvm.store) cannot be checked there: a renamed method silently
# leaves the list. Tuning only: the list changes no result, and a build without it is correct.
